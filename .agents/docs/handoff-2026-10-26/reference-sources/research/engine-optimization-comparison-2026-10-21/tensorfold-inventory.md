## Negative experiments worth retaining

The [family optimization guide](https://github.com/ashhart/TensorFold/blob/d1018b318fb51981c4a18f12e40269a4d3ee9713/docs/recipes/adding-a-zig-family.md#L195-L305) records unsuccessful persistent-wide-barrier kernels, precomputed-sum/prefetch variants, expert-union/read-once schemes, larger member groups/register spill, and speculative self/Jacobi approaches. These are model/hardware-specific failures, not universal impossibility proofs. The [Gemma MoE recipe](https://github.com/ashhart/TensorFold/blob/d1018b318fb51981c4a18f12e40269a4d3ee9713/docs/recipes/gemma-4.md) reports lost overlap from merging the dense branch with shared experts and microkernel improvements that lost at whole-model level.

Our disposition: retain the independent decomposition and fair ownership spine; test complete regions/requests before keeping a candidate. Do not blindly adopt every mechanism or assume each failed candidate stays failed on another target. A real performance win must retain exact source/state/profile and relevant physical cancellation/quarantine behavior.
